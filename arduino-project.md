Arduino Traffic Light

What I Made



For this project, me and my partner made a traffic light using a breadboard. we used a red LED, a yellow LED, a green LED, and a button. When I press the button, the lights turn on in order like a real traffic light.




Why We Chose This Idea





We chose to make a traffic light because we wanted to use multiple LEDs and a button together. We also wanted to make something based on something we see in everyday life. It was a simple way to make the different parts of the circuit work together.




New Component





The new component we used was a button. The button starts the traffic light when we press it. We searched online to figure out how to set up the circuit, and we used ChatGPT to help us when we didn't know how to write parts of the Arduino code.





What We Tried





When we first made our circuit, some of the wires were in the wrong places, so the lights were not working correctly. We checked the wires and moved them to the right places. We also had an error in our code, so we fixed it before testing the circuit again. After making those changes, the lights worked in the correct order.

Technical Tidbit: How the Button Works




The button tells the Arduino when it is pressed. The Arduino then starts the traffic light sequence. Sometimes a button can register a press more than once because of the way the button makes contact. This is called bouncing, and debouncing helps the Arduino recognize it as one press. The LEDs are outputs because the Arduino controls when each one turns on and off.
