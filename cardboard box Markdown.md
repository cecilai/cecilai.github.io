In this project, I used cardboard to create different useful objects, including a massage tool, a phone stand, and a backrest. The goal of the project was to take a simple and inexpensive material like cardboard and turn it into practical objects that could be used in everyday life. Before building each object, I had to think about its purpose and how I could shape and connect the cardboard so that it would work effectively. This required me to experiment with different folds, cuts, and ways of connecting the pieces.

One of the main challenges was making the cardboard strong and stable enough to support the objects. Since cardboard is lightweight and can bend easily, I had to think carefully about the structure and where I needed to add extra layers for support. I also learned that small changes in the design could make a big difference in how well an object worked. For example, the phone stand needed to have the right angle and enough support to keep the phone from falling over, while the backrest needed to be strong enough to support someone’s back.

Overall, this project helped me understand the importance of prototyping and testing when designing something. My first idea was not always the final solution, so I had to make adjustments and figure out what worked best. Using cardboard also showed me that materials that seem simple or limited can still be used to create useful and creative solutions when you think carefully about the design.
